## Amlodipine, migraine

**Placebo-controlled headache rates from the FDA labels** (openFDA, adverse reactions section):

| drug | headache on drug | on placebo |
|---|---|---|
| amlodipine (Norvasc), N = 1,730 vs 1,250 | 7.3% | 7.8% |
| nifedipine ER, N = 707 vs 266 | 15.8% | 9.8% |
| nisoldipine (Sular), N = 663 vs 280 | 22% | 15% |
| nicardipine IV (Cardene), N = 144 vs 100 | 15% | 2% |

**All the earlier literature on amlodipine and migraine:**
- [Dandapani 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11398308/): 2 case reports.
- [Leonard 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18053876/): 1 case report.
- [Nagai 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19057109/): 1 case report.
- [2025 pilot RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41036277/): low-dose triple combination, n = 30, −0.6 days (CI −2.9 to 1.8).

ClinicalTrials.gov has no registered trial of amlodipine alone for migraine.

**From the reviews:** 9 of 881 people taking it for blood pressure said their migraines went away, against 3 of 2,811 on blood-pressure pills with no migraine link.

## Lorcaserin and 5-HT2C agonists, sleep

**Belviq label RCT:** fatigue 7.2% vs 3.6% on placebo.

**Bexicaserin:**
- PACIFIC phase 2: somnolence was the main reason people stopped.
- Ongoing trials, none of which lists a sleep outcome:
  - [NCT06660394](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06660394) (DEEp SEA, phase 3)
  - [NCT06719141](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06719141)
  - [NCT06908226](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06908226)
  - [NCT05626634](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05626634) (open-label extension)
- The lorcaserin Dravet trial [NCT04572243](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04572243) was terminated.

**Pharmacology pointing the other way:**
- [Monti 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25932720/): a selective 5-HT2C agonist (RO-600175) increased wakefulness and cut slow-wave and REM sleep in rats.
- [Lawlor 1991](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2015332/): mCPP cut total sleep, slow-wave sleep and REM in 6 healthy volunteers.

**From the reviews:**
- 48 of 1,008 weight-loss reviewers said they slept better (verified count).
- In a wider regex pass over 63 unique sleep reviews:
  - 14 describe deeper sleep
  - 12 also mention drowsiness or fatigue
  - 5 mention night eating
  - 3 mention apnea or snoring

## Contrave, coffee

**Contrave label RCT:** insomnia 9.2% vs 5.9%, sleep disorders 13.8% vs 8.4%.

**PubMed:** no paper on bupropion + naltrexone and caffeine or coffee.

**From the reviews:**
- 10 of 2,044 Contrave reviewers lost the urge for coffee, against 1 of 4,107 on bupropion alone and 0 of 834 on naltrexone alone.
- The drop often starts in the low-dose first week.
- 4 Contrave reviewers drank more coffee to fight sleepiness.

## Progestin-only pill, migraine

**Earlier literature:**
- Desogestrel is the only progestin-only pill that has been studied for migraine, for example [Merki-Feld 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28129717/).
- PubMed has no drospirenone-only or norethindrone migraine study.

**Slynd label:** headache 2.7%, from a single-arm study.

**From the reviews:**
- Migraines got better in 3.0% of progestin-only pill reviews, against 0.8% for combined pills.
- With people switching off estrogen removed, it's 17/1,515 vs 85/20,820 (OR 2.8).
